# MOCs & Hub Notes

## Purpose
Maps of Content (MOCs) and hub notes turn folders into navigable knowledge spaces.

- `OpenClaw Hub`

## Good Practice
- Every major folder should have one entry hub.
- Hubs should summarize purpose, not just list links.
- Promote frequently used notes into hub notes.
