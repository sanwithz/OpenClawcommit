# Attachment Management

## Rules
- Store assets in `99 Attachments/`
- Prefer subfolders by year or project if the vault grows
- Keep image names descriptive
- Avoid dumping files beside notes unless the workflow really benefits

## Suggested Subfolders
- `99 Attachments/Images/`
- `99 Attachments/PDFs/`
- `99 Attachments/Exports/`
- `99 Attachments/Screenshots/`

## OpenClaw Use
OpenClaw can help summarize PDFs, organize screenshots, and generate linked note references to attachments.
